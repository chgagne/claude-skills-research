## W1 design notes

The skill does not fit the reviewer design as is:
- its calibration round (step 2), preference promotion (6) and Antidote offer (3b) need a user;
  headless runs cannot answer them, and the fresh HOME has no style profiles. W1 therefore measures
  the floor configuration (style-defaults only, no calibration) and says so;
- it fans out to subagents (global reader, section workers, noise gate): both arms get the Agent
  tool, so the comparison is about the method, not about access to subagents;
- its native outputs (ledger, annotated tex) have no counterpart without the skill: both arms end
  with the same JSON block of proposals.

Measures chosen as defaults: plant recall (5 writing plants per draft, one per type), proposal
stability (R2 span rule, tightened to 1 line because proposals are sentence-level), a scripted
harm share (numbers, cite/ref, hedge words dropped by a replacement), judged precision on a sample.
Draft `PROTOCOL-W1.md`, choices marked.

Smoke (draft 54, 1 run per arm, unscored) launched 08:50 to check that the skill runs headless,
that subagents and LaTeX work in the sandbox, and to get a cost per run for the protocol.
