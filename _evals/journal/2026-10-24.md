## Aside: Lean

The researcher asked whether Lean would give the proof skill an edge. Short reading of the 2026
literature: research-level autoformalization is below 20% on ArXivLean, statement fidelity is
unsolved, Mathlib is thin in convex optimisation, and a failed formalization cannot tell a false
step from a library gap. The likelier gain is on false alarms (proving a flagged step correct),
which Z3 already partly does. Parked until P1 closes, at the researcher's request.
