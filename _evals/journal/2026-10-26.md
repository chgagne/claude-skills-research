## 0801.4243: the documented defect holds under flatness (2026-10-28)

Before asking why 0/8 runs find "U in Lemma 2 is not a subspace", checked that it holds under the
lemma's hypothesis. Counterexample by hand, certified in Lean (`verifying-proofs/lean/L6/
U_not_subspace.lean`, CLEAN, standard axioms): K = Q, n = 1, k = 2, L(p) = p(0) + p(1) (two atoms,
B_{L_2} and B_{L_1} both rank 2, so flat; the rank step is shown by determinants, rank argued in
the comment). p1 = X^4 - X^3 and p2 = -X^4 + X^3 + X - 1/2 are in U (degree 4, only constant q
allowed, L = 0), their sum X - 1/2 is not (q = X gives 1/2). Why runs miss it: the paper asserts
"Consider the linear subspace U := ..."; every run checked the steps after it, none the claimed
closure of a set defined by a degree-dependent condition. A reading-method line ("a set defined by
a condition and called a subspace, ideal, group or cone: check closure") is a candidate, but it
would be tuned on this one paper; not written.
