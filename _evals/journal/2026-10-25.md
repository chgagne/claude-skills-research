## Decision record: how Lean ships (interview)

Asked: on request (recommended), default for serious findings (with a cost re-measure), or not at
all. Chosen: **default for serious findings**. Implemented on `lean-step` (b651504): when
`lean-certify.py --check` finds Lean, every "step is false" finding is certified, up to five per
paper, 30 minutes each; licence and notation findings need none. My reservation, for the record:
L2 showed certification adds certainty, not discovery, and costs about 5 minutes and 50k tokens per
finding; the cap bounds it. Protocol L3 (c71b284) measures the cost on the 3 stage-2 papers before
any merge. Launched detached; the usage gate held at launch (5h at 0.40).
